2Scope & the standards we follow

This policy covers personal data we collect through this website, by email, telephone, messaging apps (such as WhatsApp), and in the course of planning and delivering travel for you.

As an Egyptian travel operator we treat Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) as our baseline. Where you are protected by stricter rules, we apply those higher standards to you — in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018 for individuals in the EEA and UK, the ePrivacy rules / UK PECR for cookies, and the California CCPA/CPRA for California residents. Where these frameworks differ on a point, we apply the one most protective of the individual concerned.

3The personal data we collect

a. Information you give us

When you enquire, request a quotation, book a trip, subscribe to updates or otherwise contact us, you may provide:

  • Identity & contact details — name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, country of residence, and (for bookings) postal address.
  • Trip details — travel dates, the number and ages of travellers, destinations and interests, budget indications, special occasions, and the content of your messages to us.
  • Booking & travel-document data — where a booking requires it, traveller names as shown on passports, nationality, passport number, date of birth, and similar details needed by airlines, hotels or for visas.
  • Special requirements (special-category data) — any health, mobility, accessibility, dietary or religious information you choose to share so we can arrange suitable services. We treat this as sensitive and use it only for that purpose, on the basis of your explicit consent (see section 4).
  • Payment information — taken and processed by our payment provider(s); we do not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Marketing preferences — your consent to, or objection to, receiving updates from us.

b. Information we collect automatically

When you use the website we and our providers may collect, via cookies and similar technologies (see section 5): your IP address and approximate location, device and browser type, operating system, the pages you view and links you click, referring website, and dates and times of access.

c. Information from other sources

We may receive data about you from: payment processors (confirmation of payment, not card details), referral or affiliate partners through whom you reached us, and social-media or messaging platforms when you contact us through them — each subject to its own privacy terms.

You do not have to give us special-category or travel-document data, but without the information needed to arrange a trip we may be unable to provide the service you have asked for.

5Cookies & similar technologies

Cookies are small files placed on your device. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, tags and local storage. We use them to make the site work, to remember your preferences, and — only with your consent — to understand how the site is used and to support marketing.

Your choices

When you first visit, our consent banner lets you accept or reject each category of non-essential cookie. Non-essential cookies are not set until you agree. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time via the ["Cookie settings"] link in the footer, and through your browser settings. We honour recognised browser signals such as the Global Privacy Control where required.

6Who we share your data with

We share personal data only as needed, and never sell it. Recipients fall into these groups:

  • Travel suppliers — to deliver a trip, we pass the details necessary to the suppliers involved: hotels and lodges, airlines and transport providers, cruise and dahabiya operators, guides, restaurants, insurers and local agents, in Egypt and any other country in your itinerary. They use your data to provide their part of the trip.
  • Service providers (processors) acting on our instructions, such as: website hosting, email and CRM, analytics, payment processing, and messaging tools. Current providers include HubSpot (customer relationship management and email), Google / Gmail (email), tab.travel (travel booking and operations) and WhatsApp (messaging). Each is bound by a contract requiring appropriate protection of your data.
  • Professional advisers & authorities — accountants, lawyers, insurers, and public authorities where we are legally required to disclose, or to protect our rights.
  • Business transfers — if our business is reorganised, merged or sold, your data may transfer to the successor, subject to this policy.

7International transfers

Travel is international by nature. To arrange a trip to Egypt — or anywhere on your itinerary — your data is processed in Egypt and may be sent to suppliers in the countries you will visit. Some of our service providers may also process data outside your home country.

Where we transfer the personal data of individuals in the EEA or UK to a country without an adequacy decision (which currently includes Egypt), we put in place an appropriate safeguard recognised under the GDPR/UK GDPR — typically the Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum/IDTA where relevant) — or rely on a permitted exception, such as the transfer being necessary to perform your travel contract. You may request details of the safeguards we use by contacting us. [Confirm your actual transfer mechanisms with your adviser.]

9Your rights

Subject to the applicable law, you have the following rights over your personal data. These reflect the GDPR/UK GDPR and have close equivalents under Egypt's Law 151/2020.

Access

Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.

Rectification

Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.

Erasure

Ask us to delete your data where there's no overriding reason to keep it.

Restriction

Ask us to pause processing in certain circumstances.

Portability

Receive certain data in a portable format, or have it sent on.

Objection

Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing.

Withdraw consent

Withdraw any consent at any time, including for cookies and marketing.

Complain

Lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (see below).

To exercise any right, contact us at hello@sillage-egypte.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by law (under the GDPR, normally within one month). We may need to verify your identity first. Using your rights is free, except where requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Supervisory authorities. In Egypt, the competent authority is the [Egyptian Data Protection Centre, once operational under Law 151/2020]. In the EEA, you may complain to your local Data Protection Authority; in the UK, to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data — including encryption in transit, access controls, and limiting who can see your information to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to act promptly if anything goes wrong, including notifying you and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

11Children

Our website and services are intended for adults and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. 12Marketing choices

We send marketing only where you have agreed, or where otherwise permitted by law. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time by using it or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing does not stop service messages about a trip you have booked.

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
